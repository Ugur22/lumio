## Design Context

### Users
Fiction writers and story/roleplay hobbyists who run long creative sessions with an LLM on their own machine. They read far more than they type: replies are scenes and dialogue, often several screens long. The job is to draft, continue and refine stories while keeping the cast, lore and tone consistent (story bible, lore, summary).

### Brand Personality
Calm, literary, focused. Voice is quiet and plain, like a good writing tool: no hype, no mascot copy. The interface should feel like a desk you want to sit at for hours, with nothing shouting for attention.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial and book-like. Serif (or serif-leaning) story text with a comfortable reading measure, warmer tinted neutrals instead of cool grey, restrained accent colour used for meaning (dialogue, key moments, notes), not decoration. Light and dark themes both stay first-class. References: iA Writer and Notion (typography-led, quiet chrome). Anti-references: generic ChatGPT/Claude lookalikes, neon-on-dark AI dashboards, gradient text, glassmorphism, identical icon-card grids.

### Design Principles
1. The story is the interface: chrome recedes, reading comfort (measure, leading, type size) comes first.
2. Colour carries meaning: dialogue, actions and key moments are styled; everything else stays neutral.
3. Quiet by default, details on demand: advanced controls (providers, story bible, web, context window) stay out of the way until needed.
4. Never block the writing flow: the composer stays predictable, keyboard-first, and cannot squeeze or cover the text.
5. Accessible by default: WCAG AA contrast in both themes, visible focus, reduced-motion respected, meaning never conveyed by colour alone.
